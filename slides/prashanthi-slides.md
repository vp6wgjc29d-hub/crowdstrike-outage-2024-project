## Slide 2: What is CrowdStrike?

**CrowdStrike:**
- US Cybersecurity company, founded 2011
- Product: Falcon - Cloud antivirus
- Used by 298 Fortune 500 companies
- Protects Windows from hackers
- Runs at kernel level (deep inside Windows)
- Why it matters: If Falcon crashes, Windows crashes
